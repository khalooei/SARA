Overview

Train on the samples the model finds hardest, corrected

Deep networks are vulnerable to small input perturbations, and the samples that lie close to the decision boundary carry most of that risk. SARA revisits these samples during training. In every iteration it finds the misclassified and low-confidence samples of the mini-batch, moves each one to a nearby point that the current model classifies correctly with sufficient confidence and margin, and adds every successful correction to the batch. The correction follows the gradient of a new loss, the Confidence Guard loss, and SARA works on top of standard training or of adversarial training.

Highlights

What SARA brings

Confidence Guard loss

A cross-entropy pull toward the true class plus a hinge penalty on the margin. It upper-bounds the misclassification error and encodes the acceptance criterion exactly.

Plug-in for adversarial training

SARA wraps the base loss of standard training or of PGD, TRADES, MART and LSA adversarial training without changing it.

Up to 16 points more robust accuracy

With per-method tuning, SARA improves the robust accuracy of every base method in every configuration tested on MNIST, CIFAR10 and SVHN.

Provable inner correction

Under a standard smoothness assumption, the correction converges to a stationary point inside the admissible set, and every accepted sample certifies a robust neighborhood.

Class-count-relative threshold

The confidence threshold is set relative to chance level, ξ · 10 / C, so that the acceptance criterion stays meaningful on CIFAR100 and Tiny-ImageNet-200.

Efficient and controllable

All challenging samples are corrected in one batched operation, and a correction budget and a confidence cap address the training instabilities analyzed in the paper.

Method

One training iteration

  1. Identify

    Collect the challenging set S: samples that are misclassified or whose true-class probability is below ξ.

  2. Correct

    Run at most κ projected gradient steps on the Confidence Guard loss inside the L∞ ball of radius ε and the valid pixel range.

  3. Accept

    Keep a corrected sample once its confidence reaches ξ and its margin is at most −τ. The others are discarded.

  4. Train

    Update the model on the base loss over the batch plus the Confidence Guard loss over the accepted corrections.

Challenging setS = {(x, y) ∈ B : argmaxj fj(x) ≠ y or fy(x) < ξ}
Marginh(x, y) = maxj≠y fj(x) − fy(x)
Confidence Guard lossLCG(x, y) = CE(f(x), y) + λmargin · ReLU(h(x, y) + τ)
Inner updateδ ← ΠΔ(δ − α ∇δ LCG(x + δ, y)), Δ = {δ : ‖δ‖∞ ≤ ε, x + δ ∈ [0, 1]d}
Outer objectiveLossSARA = Lossbase(B) + LossCG(S*)

Theory

Guarantees behind the correction

Convergence

Every iterate stays in the admissible set. Under a standard smoothness assumption, the Confidence Guard loss decreases monotonically and the correction reaches an approximate stationary point at rate O(1/K).

Consistency

The Confidence Guard loss upper-bounds the misclassification indicator, and its two terms encode the confidence and margin conditions of the acceptance region exactly.

Certified neighborhood

An accepted sample is surrounded by a ball of radius min{r, τ / Lh} on which the prediction does not change.

Vicinal risk minimization

The outer objective adds a model-dependent vicinity concentrated near the decision boundary to the empirical risk.

Get started

Install and train

git clone https://github.com/khalooei/SARA.git
cd SARA
pip install -e .

python train.py --dataset mnist --model lenet --base-loss pgd --sara \
    --epochs 20 --eval-apgd --out-dir runs/mnist/at_pgd_sara

MNIST, CIFAR10, CIFAR100 and SVHN download automatically. The repository includes the scripts of every experiment, the GAIRAT, FAT and OHEM baselines, and unit tests.
